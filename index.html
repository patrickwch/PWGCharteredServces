Getting picked up at Melbourne Airport墨尔本机场接机指引

Private cars can no longer collect you at the kerb outside Terminals 1, 2 and 3. If someone is picking you up, walk to the free Pick Up zone on Level 2 of the T123 Transport Hub, about 285 metres from the terminal. Taxis, Uber and SkyBus still leave from the ground-level forecourt.私家车已不能在 1、2、3 号航站楼门外的路边接人。如果有人来接您，请步行前往 T123 交通枢纽（T123 Transport Hub）2 层的免费接客区（Pick Up），距航站楼约 285 米。出租车、Uber 和 SkyBus 机场大巴仍在地面层前广场上车。

The walk to the Pick Up zone步行前往接客区

Allow 5 to 10 minutes with luggage (our estimate). There are rest stops every 60 metres. Before you leave the arrivals hall, message your driver that you are through.携带行李约需 5 至 10 分钟（我方估算）。沿途每 60 米设有休息点。离开到达大厅前，请先发消息告诉司机您已经出来了。

  1. 1

    Cross the forecourt穿过前广场

    Leave the arrivals hall at ground level and use the pedestrian crossings towards the building signed “T123 Transport Hub”. Do not wait at the kerb: private cars cannot stop there.从地面层走出到达大厅，沿人行横道走向标有“T123 Transport Hub”的建筑。请勿在路边等候：私家车不能在此停靠。

    Enter the T123 Transport Hub进入 T123 交通枢纽

    Walk in under the large “T123 Transport Hub” sign.从“T123 Transport Hub”大型标牌下方进入。

    Take a lift to Level 2乘电梯到 2 层

    The lifts are just inside. Press 2.电梯就在入口内侧。按“2”。

  4. 4

    Leave the lift and turn left出电梯后左转

    Follow the yellow line on the floor.沿地面黄线前行。

  5. 5

    Turn right at the green sign在绿色标牌处右转

    The green sign reads “T123 Pick-up”, and the yellow floor arrow bends right here.绿色标牌上写着“T123 Pick-up”，地面的黄色箭头在此向右转。

    Go straight along the walkway沿步行通道直行

Arriving at Terminal 4抵达 4 号航站楼

Nothing has changed at Terminal 4. Private cars pick up on Level 1 of the T4 Transport Hub, the car park directly beside the terminal. Taxis and Uber pick up on its ground level.4 号航站楼没有变化。私家车在 T4 交通枢纽（T4 Transport Hub）1 层接人，即紧邻航站楼的停车楼。出租车和 Uber 在其地面层上车。

Virgin Australia passengers can also choose to be collected here: the airport notes the T4 Pick Up zone can be easier for Terminal 3 arrivals. Agree the meeting point with your driver before you land.维珍澳航的旅客也可以选择在这里上车：机场表示，从 3 号航站楼到达的旅客使用 T4 接客区可能更方便。请在落地前与司机约好碰面地点。

Not being collected?没有人接机？

Taxi, Uber, rideshare and SkyBus出租车、Uber、网约车和 SkyBus

These have not moved. They still pick up at ground level, close to the arrivals doors.这些上车点没有变动，仍在地面层，靠近到达大厅出口。

  • Taxi: use only the signed taxi ranks at Terminals 1, 2 and 4. The city is about a 30-minute ride.出租车：只使用 1、2、4 号航站楼有标识的出租车候车站。到市区车程约 30 分钟。
  • Uber: ranks are at Terminal 1 (turn left out of the terminal), Terminal 2 (kerb in front of the terminal) and the ground floor of the Terminal 4 car park.Uber：上车点位于 1 号航站楼（出航站楼左转）、2 号航站楼（航站楼前路边）以及 4 号航站楼停车场地面层。
  • DiDi, Shebah and other rideshare: Lane 3 of the forecourt for Terminals 1, 2 and 3; Level 1 of the Terminal 4 car park for Terminal 4.DiDi、Shebah 及其他网约车：1、2、3 号航站楼在前广场 3 号车道（Lane 3）；4 号航站楼在 4 号航站楼停车场 1 层。
  • SkyBus to the city (Southern Cross Station): stops outside Terminals 1, 3 and 4, running 4am to 1am, every 10 to 15 minutes.SkyBus 前往市区（南十字星车站 Southern Cross Station）：站点在 1、3、4 号航站楼门外，运营时间为凌晨 4 点至次日凌晨 1 点，每 10 至 15 分钟一班。
  • Pre-booked chauffeur: your driver meets you at the chauffeur meeting point inside the terminal.预约专车：司机会在航站楼内的专车接客点（Chauffeur meeting point）等您。

Do not accept a ride from anyone who approaches you inside or outside the terminal. Check the number plate and driver photo in your app before getting in.不要搭乘在航站楼内外主动上前揽客的车辆。上车前请在手机应用中核对车牌和司机照片。

If you need help with the walk如果步行有困难

For the person collecting you给接机人的提示

  • Take Exit 19 (Naarm Way) from the Tullamarine Freeway and follow the signs to T123 Pick Up (Level 2) or T4 Pick Up (Level 1).从 Tullamarine Freeway 高速公路 19 号出口（Exit 19, Naarm Way）驶出，按路牌前往 T123 Pick Up（2 层）或 T4 Pick Up（1 层）。
  • The Pick Up zone is free, with a 5-minute limit. Enter only once your passenger is standing there, and tell them your zone number.接客区免费，限停 5 分钟。请等乘客到位后再驶入，并把分区编号告诉对方。
  • Arriving early? The wait zone on Mercer Drive is free for 60 minutes and a 5-minute drive from the Pick Up zone.到早了？Mercer Drive 的等候区（Wait Zone）前 60 分钟免费，开到接客区约 5 分钟。
  • To meet a passenger inside the terminal, park in the T123 car park. The first 15 minutes are free.如需进航站楼接人，请停在 T123 停车场，前 15 分钟免费。
  • Vehicles over 2.2 metres high cannot enter the T123 zone (2.5 metres at T4). Use Exit 20, Terminal Drive, then Centre Road to the ground-level bay behind the T123 Transport Hub.车高超过 2.2 米的车辆不能进入 T123 接客区（T4 为 2.5 米）。请走 20 号出口（Exit 20），经 Terminal Drive 转 Centre Road，到 T123 交通枢纽后方的地面层停靠点。
